\section{Worked uniformity examples}
The conditions are also tight, and the two paths really do diverge when they fail. Take $m=2$, $\ell=2$, $A = \{0,2\}$: $m \mid 2^\ell$ holds, so the compiled path stays uniform at $(50\%, 50\%)$, whereas the pooled path folds the residue-1 draws onto $a_1 = 2$ and emits $(25\%, 75\%)$; the accompanying test suite pins both the prediction and the empirical split. This is the precise sense in which a declarative layout's field width and admissible set are correctness obligations on the author, not tuning parameters. A declaration that skips residue classes is still \emph{admissible} on every draw, but its per-value frequencies are path-dependent and the validator reports the residual bound rather than silently accepting it.

For database-key ($m=5$, $\ell=8$), $256 \bmod 5 = 1$, so one residue class holds 52 raw values and the other four hold 51; with the contiguous set $A = \{1,2,3,4,5\}$ the class of index~0 (value~1) is the heavy one, giving $(52,51,51,51,51)$ preimages out of 256, a maximum per-value deviation of $(52 - 51.2)/256 \approx 0.31\%$ from uniform $51.2/256$, and a worst-case spread between classes of $(52-51)/256 = 0.39\%$. For multi-tenant, the region field ($m=4$, $\ell=8$) and the tenant field ($m=8$, $\ell=12$) both satisfy $m \mid 2^\ell$ with $G_i = 1$ throughout, so both are exactly uniform on either path.

The asymptotic advantage is real but bounded, and its limits are stated where it begins. for $k=1$ the crossover falls between $d=0.016$ and $d=0.031$ (adjacent

\section{Smith optimality proof}
Smith swap proof. $D$ is minimised by ordering fields in non-increasing $w_i/\ell_i$: swapping an adjacent inverted pair changes $D$ by $\ell_a \ell_b (w_a/\ell_a - w_b/\ell_b) \le 0$.

\section{Family-wise uniformity assessment}
Across all 17 tested cells the family-wise assessment gives max-$|z|=2.90$ against a Bonferroni critical value of $2.97$ at family-wise $\alpha=0.05$ (\v{S}id\'ak $2.97$, Bonferroni $p=0.064$), i.e.\ consistent with sampling noise once the 17-fold multiple comparison is accounted for.
